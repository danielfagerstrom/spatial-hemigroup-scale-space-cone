part, a \Levy{} measure on $(0,\infty)$ and a nonnegative drift, with the Laplace exponent
$\gamma_0u + \int(1 - e^{-ux})\,\nu(dx)$ (\sscite{sato1999levy}, Thm.~24.11, p.~153, and
